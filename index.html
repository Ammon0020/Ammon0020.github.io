ABOUT

I design, photograph, and build things — digital and physical. My work spans 3D modeling for fabrication, photography, custom hardware, and IT infrastructure. I'm not a specialist; I'm a maker who moves fluidly between disciplines, driven by the conviction that understanding how things work at every layer produces better outcomes than staying in one lane.

Whether it's fabricating a custom PCB for a flight simulator controller, engineering a multi-camera surveillance network, or designing 3D-printed enclosures for routers and controllers — the through-line is precision, craft, and a refusal to accept "good enough."
