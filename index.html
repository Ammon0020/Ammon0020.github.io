FLIGHT SIM CONTROLLER

Custom PCB design & fabrication for precision flight simulation input

FILE://PROJ_001 STATUS: ACTIVE

PROJECT LOG: FLIGHT SIM CONTROLLER

INITIATED: 2025.03 — STATUS: ACTIVE

This project started with a simple frustration: commercial flight sim controllers lack the precision and tactile feedback that serious sim pilots need. Rather than compromise, I designed a custom solution from the ground up.

The controller uses a custom-designed PCB fabricated with KiCad, featuring hall-effect sensors for analog axis inputs and mechanical switches for toggle functions. The enclosure was 3D-modeled in Fusion 360 and printed in PETG for durability. The firmware runs on an Arduino Pro Micro, presenting itself as a native USB HID joystick device.

The build involved multiple prototyping iterations — from breadboard testing through to final assembly with custom wiring harnesses and soldered connections. Every component was chosen for reliability and feel.

CHURCH SURVEILLANCE SYSTEM

Multi-camera IP network for comprehensive facility monitoring

FILE://PROJ_002 STATUS: DEPLOYED

PROJECT LOG: CHURCH SURVEILLANCE SYSTEM

INITIATED: 2024.08 — STATUS: DEPLOYED

Designed and deployed a comprehensive IP camera surveillance network for a large church facility. The project required careful planning of camera placement, network infrastructure, and storage solutions to ensure 24/7 coverage across multiple buildings.

The system features PoE-powered cameras running on a dedicated VLAN, with a centralized NVR providing 30 days of continuous recording. I configured motion-triggered alerts, remote viewing access for facility managers, and implemented proper network segmentation to isolate the surveillance traffic from the church's general-use network.

The deployment included running structured cabling, mounting cameras at optimal angles, and configuring the recording schedules to balance storage capacity with coverage needs.

CUSTOM ROUTER BUILD

Bespoke network infrastructure — routing, firewalling, traffic shaping

FILE://PROJ_003 STATUS: OPERATIONAL

PROJECT LOG: CUSTOM ROUTER BUILD

INITIATED: 2024.05 — STATUS: OPERATIONAL

Built a custom router/firewall appliance from commodity hardware to replace a consumer-grade router that couldn't keep up with growing network demands. The build runs pfSense on a small-form-factor PC with multiple Intel NICs.

Configuration includes multi-WAN failover, VLAN segmentation for IoT devices, guest networks, and production traffic, along with Suricata IDS/IPS for intrusion detection. I implemented QoS policies for traffic shaping, DNS-level ad blocking via pfBlockerNG, and WireGuard VPN for secure remote access.

The enclosure is a custom 3D-printed case designed to maximize airflow while maintaining a clean, compact footprint. The project demonstrates the intersection of IT infrastructure knowledge and hands-on fabrication.

3D PRINT DESIGN

Custom enclosure design and 3D modeling for routers, controllers, and computer cases

FILE://PROJ_004 STATUS: ACTIVE

PROJECT LOG: 3D PRINT DESIGN

INITIATED: 2023.11 — STATUS: ACTIVE

An ongoing collection of functional 3D-printed enclosures and components designed for real-world use. Every design starts with precise measurements and constraints — these aren't decorative prints, they're engineered solutions.

Projects include custom router enclosures with integrated cable management, controller housings with ventilation channels, and modular mounting systems for networking equipment. Each design goes through multiple iterations in Fusion 360, with test prints to validate fit and thermal performance before final production.

Materials are selected based on application requirements: PETG for heat resistance near electronics, PLA for rapid prototyping, and TPU for vibration-dampening mounts. Print settings are tuned for each material and use case.

ARCHITECTURAL SERIES

Capturing structure, light, and material in the built environment

FILE://PROJ_005 STATUS: ONGOING

PROJECT LOG: ARCHITECTURAL SERIES

INITIATED: 2024.01 — STATUS: ONGOING

An ongoing photographic exploration of the built environment — focusing on the interplay of structure, light, material, and negative space in architectural subjects. Shot primarily in urban and suburban settings.

The series emphasizes strong geometric composition, dramatic lighting conditions (golden hour, blue hour, and harsh midday contrast), and post-processing that enhances material texture without sacrificing authenticity. I shoot primarily with natural light, using manual settings to control depth of field and motion.

Post-processing is handled in Lightroom with a focus on tonal accuracy and controlled color grading. The goal is images that feel intentional and precise — the same philosophy I apply to building things.
