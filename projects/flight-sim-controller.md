This project started with a simple frustration: commercial flight sim controllers lack the precision and tactile feedback that serious sim pilots need. Rather than compromise, I designed a custom solution from the ground up.

The controller uses a custom-designed PCB fabricated with KiCad, featuring hall-effect sensors for analog axis inputs and mechanical switches for toggle functions. The enclosure was 3D-modeled in Fusion 360 and printed in PETG for durability. The firmware runs on an Arduino Pro Micro, presenting itself as a native USB HID joystick device.

The build involved multiple prototyping iterations — from breadboard testing through to final assembly with custom wiring harnesses and soldered connections. Every component was chosen for reliability and feel.
