Built a custom router/firewall appliance from commodity hardware to replace a consumer-grade router that couldn't keep up with growing network demands. The build runs pfSense on a small-form-factor PC with multiple Intel NICs.

Configuration includes multi-WAN failover, VLAN segmentation for IoT devices, guest networks, and production traffic, along with Suricata IDS/IPS for intrusion detection. I implemented QoS policies for traffic shaping, DNS-level ad blocking via pfBlockerNG, and WireGuard VPN for secure remote access.

The enclosure is a custom 3D-printed case designed to maximize airflow while maintaining a clean, compact footprint. The project demonstrates the intersection of IT infrastructure knowledge and hands-on fabrication.
